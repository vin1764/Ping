# Ping: the app

**Live:** [https://vin1764.github.io/ping-dashboard/](https://vin1764.github.io/ping-dashboard/)

## Two ways in

- **Tap View demo** for a quick look, no login: an anonymised snapshot of a real run (real companies, roles, pay and
  deadlines; a made-up student; no one's personal data), read-only.
- **Sign in** with the credentials in the submission note to see my live data.

## Six things to try

1. **"what's due tonight?"** Applications closing tonight, *For you* first with the CV to apply with.
2. **"any tests today?"** then **"anything else?"** The second question is understood from the first.
3. **"which cv for blackbuck"** The CV that fits that role best.
4. **Today** tab: tap a row for everything in it, with links that open the ERP, the form or the notice.
5. **Forms** tab: every form from the notices and mail, one row each; tick one as filled and its reminders stop.
6. **Reply to a message:** swipe a chat bubble to the right (or use its arrow on a computer) and ask
   "which of these haven't I applied to?" on a deadline digest.

Ping only reads the ERP: ask it to "apply for me" and it says it can't.

## Install it on a phone

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link, tap the **⋮** menu, then **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).

It opens like an app, keeps you signed in, and can send notifications when something is for you.
